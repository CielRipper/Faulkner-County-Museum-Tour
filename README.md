# Faulkner-County-Museum-Tour

A self-guided museum tour app for the Faulkner County Museum, built in partnership with Ree Walker (President, Faulkner County Historical Society).

# Team Contract

This contract is the governing document for our team. It establishes our structure, procedures, expectations of participation, accountability, and consequences for breach of contract. Any changes to this contract require the unanimous consent of all members.

## Communication

- Our main channel is our Teams group chat. Project-wide decisions and updates go there so everyone sees them.
- Members should respond to messages within **24 hours** on weekdays. If you will be unavailable (sick, traveling, exams), let the team know as early as you can.
- All communication with the client goes through the Client Interfacer, with the rest of the team and professor Dr. Goadrich CC'd on emails.

## Meetings

- We will meet at least **once a week**, in person. Tuesdays and Thursdays before 4 PM are our default times.
- If you can't make a meeting, tell the team beforehand and catch up on what was discussed afterward.
- Client meetings are scheduled around the client's availability first. We try to have everyone attend, but won't hold up a meeting if most of us can make it.

## Decision-Making

- Decisions affecting the project are made by **unanimous consent**. Every member works toward agreement and compromise rather than outvoting each other.
- If we can't reach consensus after discussing it, we will write down the steps we've taken and a proposal for moving forward, then ask the professor to moderate.

## Task Management & Workflow

- Work is tracked with GitHub Issues / Projects. Each task has an owner and a target date.
- No one tries to push directly to `main` for feature work. Use a branch, open a pull request, and have at least one other member review it before merging.
- Write clear commit messages so the history shows who did what.

## Expectations of Participation

Each member agrees to:
- Contribute a fair share of the work across code, testing, design, and documentation.
- Complete assigned tasks by their deadlines, or ask for help / a new deadline **before** the deadline passes.
- Come to meetings prepared and be respectful of everyone's ideas and time.
- Be professional and respectful with the client, who is volunteering their time to work with us.

## Accountability

- At each weekly meeting we review progress on open tasks.
- If someone is falling behind, the team checks in with them first to see what's going on and how we can help.
- Contributions are visible through GitHub commits, pull requests, and issues.

## Conflict Resolution

1. Bring the issue up directly and respectfully with the person involved.
2. If it isn't resolved, discuss it as a full team at the next meeting.
3. If the team still can't agree, contact the professor with what we've tried and a proposed solution.

## Consequences for Breach of Contract

1. **First issue:** a private conversation with the team to understand the problem and agree on a plan to fix it.
2. **Repeated issues:** a written note in the team chat documenting the problem and the agreed plan.
3. **Continued issues:** the team contacts the professor, and peer evaluations will reflect each member's actual contribution.
